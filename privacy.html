Gym Tracker privacy

You can use Gym Tracker without an account. Then everything stays on your phone.

If you make an account

Your email, and the things you log (workouts, food, body weight, sleep, to-dos, schedule and settings) are stored on Google Firebase, with the database in London, so they can follow you to another phone. Some of this is health information, so only make an account if you are happy with that.

Who can see it

The database only lets your own login read your data. The person who runs this app could technically open it. Nothing is sold or used for ads.

What friends see

Friends you accept see your username and your ranks, nothing else. You can hide your ranks in Settings. Anyone with an account can check if a username exists and send a request.

Food search

Online food searches go to Open Food Facts.

Deleting it

Settings, then Delete account, removes your login, saved copy, username and friends list. Data on your phone stays until you remove the app. If someone has blocked you, their block record stays.

Questions

Ask the person who gave you the app.
